# Design input and probability meaning

[English](schema.md) · [中文](schema.zh-CN.md)

The optional JSON input passed with `--spec` contains four illustrative parameters and exactly these five paths, in order: `A1_F1_U1`, `A1_F0_U1`, `A0_F1_U0`, `A0_F0_U1`, `A0_F0_U0`. `A` is the buyer's **initial choice**, `F` indicates a **maintained contract requirement**, and `U` is the **ultimate choice**; 1 means accept or maintained as applicable. The source figure omits later decision nodes where buyers in its sample did not change their initial choice: initially accepted buyers ultimately accepted under either contract condition, and initially rejected buyers ultimately rejected when the requirement was maintained. This is an observed-sample simplification, **not a logical restriction of the experimental design**. The template fixes those five source-figure leaves and only draws a further accept/reject node for `A=0,F=0`.

`p_initial_accept` is the illustrative share/probability of buyers initially accepting; initial choice is **not** randomized in the source. `p_contract_maintained_given_initial_accept` and `p_contract_maintained_given_initial_reject` must be equal because the source randomizes the requirement independently of initial choice. They are conditional on the respective `A` branch; the common value is an illustrative randomization probability, not one supplied by Figure 1. `p_ultimate_accept_given_initial_reject_and_lifted` is conditional on **both** initial rejection and the requirement being lifted; it represents illustrative subsequent behavior, not a randomized assignment. All probabilities must be finite in `[0,1]`.

The exported `illustrative_leaf_probabilities.csv` gives each path's joint probability, its conditional final-branch denominator, and ultimate payment branch. For example `P(A=0,F=0,U=1) = (1-p_initial_accept) × (1-p_contract_maintained_given_initial_reject) × p_ultimate_accept_given_initial_reject_and_lifted`. All five path probabilities sum to one. The figure itself intentionally has no numerical probabilities because the source Figure 1 provides none. The synthetic probabilities demonstrate path accounting only; they are not estimates or source values.
