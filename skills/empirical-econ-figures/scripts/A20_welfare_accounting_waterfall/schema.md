# Input contract

[English](schema.md) · [中文](schema.zh-CN.md)

UTF-8 CSV columns: `group,ledger,seq,item,kind,value`. One row is one ordered step in one monetary ledger for one group. The current configuration has two group IDs (`group_a`, `group_b`) and two ledger IDs (`revenue`, `wtp`); edit their bilingual labels and item vocabulary in both scripts for another application. Every group must have the same ordered steps within a ledger. `seq` starts at 1 without gaps. `value` is a finite monetary amount in **US dollars per additional $1 of audit spending** in the demo. All amount rows, including subtotals and totals, share this unit.

`kind=component` adds signed `value` to that ledger's running balance and is drawn as a floating bar from the old to new balance. `kind=subtotal` repeats the **current** balance as a bar from zero and must equal the sum of preceding components. `kind=total` does the same for the final balance; exactly one is required per group–ledger and it must be last. Subtotal and total values are checked, never added a second time. The configured revenue total item is `net_revenue`; the WTP total is `net_wtp`. Missing or nonnumeric amounts, duplicate items, nonconsecutive steps, inconsistent group layouts, unknown IDs, unbalanced subtotals/totals, and a nonpositive net-revenue denominator are rejected. The script outputs `_checked.csv` with `bottom,top,running` and `_ratios.csv` with both total amounts and dimensionless `mvpf`.

For the source-motivated audit example, `net_revenue = audit_cost + upfront_revenue + deterrence_revenue`, where `audit_cost` is entered as a **negative** signed amount. `net_wtp = upfront_taxes + deterrence_taxes + response_burden` in this synthetic illustration; the tax components and response burden are positive willingness-to-pay-to-avoid-audit amounts. The displayed `MVPF = net_wtp / net_revenue` is a separate **dimensionless ratio** and never a money bar. The demo's intermediate revenue subtotal is an illustration of the supported subtotal type and does not appear in the cited original Figure VIII.
